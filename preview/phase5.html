Phase 5: Results screen

After the 15th answer, the app now shows a result instead of the holding page. The score comes from the scoring rules built in Phase 3.

What the learner sees

  • A large status: green "Ready to book", amber "Almost there", or red "Not yet", plus one sentence.
  • A bar and a percentage for vocabulary, grammar, and reading.
  • Up to three things to practise, or a short well-done message if nothing was weak.
  • A reminder that the real exam also has listening, writing, and speaking.
  • A button, "Take the check again", which starts over from question 1.

Four tries worth making

  • All correct: Ready to book.
  • All wrong: Not yet.
  • First 9 correct and the last 6 wrong: Almost there.
  • Vocabulary and reading perfect, grammar mostly wrong: Almost there, even though the overall score looks high.
