Phase 6: Design

The check now looks like a simple phone-friendly page. The questions, the scoring, and the buttons do the same things as before.

What changed in the look

  • A white card on a calm background, with rounded corners and space around the words.
  • One main colour, a deep teal, for the title and the main buttons.
  • Answer buttons that are large enough to tap, with the chosen one filled in teal.
  • The same layout on a phone and on a wider screen. The wider screen just adds room around the card.
