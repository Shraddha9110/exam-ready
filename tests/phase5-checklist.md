# Phase 5 manual checklist

Open `index.html` in your browser. Finish all 15 questions, then read the results page.

Correct answers, if you need them:

1. Stift
2. 7:15
3. Onkel
4. in der Bäckerei
5. Guten Abend

7. wohnst

10. Die
11. in Berlin
12. von neun bis zwölf Uhr
13. mit Lea
14. Sie lernt zu Hause.
15. Maria erzählt Anna von ihrem Alltag in Berlin.

A wrong answer means: pick the first choice, unless that choice is the correct one. Then pick the second choice.

## 1. All correct

Steps:
1. Click Start.
2. Choose the correct answer for every question, then click Next.
3. Read the results page.

Expected result:
Status is **Ready to book**, in large green text, with a sentence saying your vocabulary, grammar and reading are strong enough to book the exam. The bars show Vocabulary 100%, Grammar 100%, and Reading 100%. Under "What to practise before booking" you see a short well-done message and no practice tips. The note about listening, writing and speaking is on the page. A button says "Take the check again".

## 2. All wrong

Steps:
1. Click Take the check again.
2. Choose a wrong answer for every question, then click Next.
3. Read the results page.

Expected result:
Status is **Not yet**, in large red text, with a sentence saying to keep practising before booking. Every bar shows 0%. Three practice lines appear, most mistakes first: finding details, everyday words, and articles (der/die/das). Each line includes its practice tip.

## 3. Mixed attempt

Steps:
1. Click Take the check again.
2. Answer questions 1 to 9 correctly.
3. Answer questions 10 to 15 with a wrong choice.
4. Read the results page.

Expected result:
Status is **Almost there**, in large amber text, with a sentence saying you are close and should practise the weak spots. The bars show Vocabulary 100%, Grammar 80%, and Reading 0%. Practice lines appear for the weak spots, with finding details first.

## 4. Per-skill case

Steps:
1. Click Take the check again.
2. Answer all five vocabulary questions correctly (questions 1 to 5).
3. On grammar, answer only question 6 correctly (Das). Answer questions 7, 8, 9, and 10 with a wrong choice.
4. Answer all five reading questions correctly (questions 11 to 15).
5. Read the results page.

Expected result:
Status is **Almost there**, not "Ready to book", in large amber text. The bars show Vocabulary 100%, Grammar 20%, and Reading 100%. Practice lines name the weak grammar spots. A strong vocabulary and reading score does not hide the weak grammar score.

## 5. Restart button

Steps:
1. From any results page, click Take the check again.
2. Look at question 1 and do not choose an answer yet.

Expected result:
The results page hides. Question 1 of 15 is shown, Next is switched off, and no answer is marked. The old result is gone. A new run starts from an empty set of answers.
