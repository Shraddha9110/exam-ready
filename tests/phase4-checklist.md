# Phase 4 manual checklist

Open `index.html` in your browser. Do these checks in order.

## 1. Start button

Steps:
1. Open `index.html`.
2. Read the first screen.
3. Click Start.

Expected result:
The first screen shows the title "Am I exam-ready?", one line about 15 questions, about 5 minutes, and vocabulary, grammar and reading, plus a Start button. After you click Start, that screen hides and question 1 appears.

## 2. Progress counter

Steps:
1. On question 1, read the progress line.
2. Choose any answer and click Next.
3. Read the progress line again.

Expected result:
Question 1 says "Question 1 of 15". After Next, it says "Question 2 of 15".

## 3. Next disabled before selecting

Steps:
1. Look at the Next button as soon as a new question appears.
2. Try to move on without choosing an answer.

Expected result:
Next is greyed out and does nothing until you choose an answer. After you choose one, Next can be clicked.

## 4. Selected option highlighted

Steps:
1. Click one answer.
2. Click a different answer.

Expected result:
The answer you clicked has a blue outline and bold text. When you click another answer, the highlight moves to that one and leaves the first.

## 5. Reading text only on reading questions

Steps:
1. Look at a vocabulary question (questions 1 to 5) and a grammar question (questions 6 to 10).
2. Move to question 11, the first reading question.
3. Stay through question 15.

Expected result:
Questions 1 to 10 do not show Maria's email. Questions 11 to 15 show the email above the question. The email starts with "Liebe Anna" and mentions Berlin.

## 6. All 15 questions reachable

Steps:
1. From question 1, choose an answer and click Next.
2. Repeat until you reach question 15.

Expected result:
You can see every question from 1 to 15. The question text changes each time, and the progress line counts up to "Question 15 of 15".

## 7. Results screen appears at the end

Steps:
1. On question 15, choose an answer.
2. Click Next.

Expected result:
The question hides. A screen appears with the text "Results coming soon".
