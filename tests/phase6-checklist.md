# Phase 6 manual checklist

Open `index.html` in your browser. The behaviour of the quiz should be the same as before. This check is only about how the page looks.

## 1. Desktop view

Steps:
1. Open `index.html` in a wide browser window.
2. Read the welcome page.
3. Click Start, choose an answer, and look at the question page.
4. Finish the check, or restart a finished check, and look at the results page.

Expected result:
The page sits in a white rounded card in the middle of a soft grey-green background. The title and main buttons use one deep teal colour. There is clear space around the words. The chosen answer is filled with a light teal and a teal border. Results still use green, amber, and red for the three statuses.

## 2. Phone view

Steps:
1. Open Chrome DevTools with Cmd+Option+I.
2. Click the phone icon (device mode).
3. Pick a phone, such as iPhone SE or a similar narrow width.
4. Look at the welcome page, one question, and the results page.

Expected result:
The card uses the width of the phone with a small margin. Text stays large enough to read. Buttons stack in one column. Nothing is cut off at the side, and you do not have to scroll sideways.

## 3. Buttons easy to tap

Steps:
1. Stay in the phone view.
2. Look at Start, each answer, Next, and Take the check again.
3. Tap an answer, then tap a different answer.

Expected result:
Every button is tall and wide enough for a thumb. The answer you tap is clearly marked. Next looks grey and cannot be used until an answer is chosen, then it turns teal.

## 4. Text readable

Steps:
1. On the phone view, read the welcome line, a question, Maria's email on a reading question, and the results sentence.

Expected result:
Words are dark on a white or very light background. The question is larger than the progress line. The email keeps its line breaks and does not run off the card.

## 5. Nothing overflowing the screen

Steps:
1. On the phone view, scroll the welcome page, a question with four long answers, a reading question, and the results page.
2. Watch the edges of the screen while you scroll.

Expected result:
The card, the buttons, the email, and the score bars stay inside the screen. There is no sideways scrollbar.
